# OpenClaw task examples

Use these as natural-language commands for an OpenClaw agent connected to the GeoLibre Location Intelligence service.

## Territory planning

"Map our Lagos prospect territories, rank them by lead density, and identify the three areas we should prioritize next month."

## Campaign coverage

"Show which territories within 10 km of our target customer clusters have low campaign coverage. Return the top five gaps."

## Site selection

"Compare these candidate locations by nearby prospect count, distance to existing branches, and territory coverage. Rank them."

## Client reporting

"Generate a map-backed summary of this client's lead distribution and identify the strongest geographic opportunity."

## Field work

"Create a field-sales route through the highest-value nearby prospect clusters and return the stops in priority order."
